# Questro Community Guidelines

Help make Questro a welcoming place for feedback and collaboration.

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Discuss problems and proposed changes constructively.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.
- Do not share another person's private information or account data.
- Keep issues and discussions relevant to the project.

These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation when necessary.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/bitlukaa). If no private contact channel is available, request one without publicly posting sensitive details.
